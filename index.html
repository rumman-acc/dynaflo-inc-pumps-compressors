About Dynaflo

Dynaflo, Inc. was founded in 1998 in Pennsylvania after identifying a need in the medical equipment industry for smaller, quieter, and more efficient vacuum pumps and compressors. The company designs, engineers, manufactures, inspects, assembles, and tests all products on-site in Pennsylvania with high-quality, precision materials.

Dynaflo employs approximately 18 people and is led by President & CEO Kevin Shaw. The company's cutting-edge designs have been employed in industrial, biomedical, and air quality applications.

In 2008, Dynaflo's custom engineered 3000 Series radial diaphragm air compressor was named a finalist in Design News' "Build a Better Mousetrap" competition, recognizing the innovation and quality that defines our products.

Products & Services

Dynaflo manufactures several series of custom diaphragm pumps designed for demanding applications. All pumps can be fully customized including motor type, performance specifications, materials, wiring, connectors, and mounting specifications.

1000 Series

Small, powerful diaphragm pumps designed for applications requiring reliable vacuum and pressure generation.

Max Flow: 75 L/min
Operating Voltage: 12 VDC or 24 VDC
Applications: Air sampling, medical aspirators, vacuum lifters

2000 Series

Micro diaphragm pumps engineered for precision applications requiring compact, efficient performance.

Max Flow: 1.15 L/min
Applications: Suction-based prosthetics, drug delivery systems, aerospace applications

3000 Series "Radial 12"

Smooth flow diaphragm pumps providing the only true pulse-free output flow on the market for critical ventilator applications.

Max Flow: 140 L/min
Applications: Mechanical ventilators requiring pulse-free airflow
Recognition: 2008 Design News "Build a Better Mousetrap" finalist

6000 Series

Mini diaphragm pumps specifically designed for precision drug infusion systems.

Applications: Drug infusion systems
